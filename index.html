Who’s who, and how to be

Tap the speaker to hear each phrase.

Quick grammar: A noun names a person, place, thing, or idea. A pronoun replaces a noun. The subject is who or what a sentence is about. A verb expresses an action or a state of being.

When do we use ser?

A little language context

Vosotros/vosotras is informal “you all” in most of Spain. Ustedes is “you all” across Latin America and formal in Spain.

You may see elle, elles, nosotres, and vosotres as inclusive forms. Usage varies; elle takes es and elles takes son.

Spanish usually leaves out a subject pronoun for “it.” Say “Es un libro” (It is a book). For people, “they” can be ellos, ellas, or inclusive elles.
